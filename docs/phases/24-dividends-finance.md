# Phase 24 — Dividends & Finance Integration

**Depends on:** 17
**Complexity:** M

## Goal
The market and club finances become one connected economy, as originally designed.

## Tasks
- [ ] Dividend calculation from squad share performance
- [ ] Club budget/wage-bill model, dividend income feeding available budget
- [ ] Finance UI on the dashboard reflecting real dividend income, not placeholder numbers

## Explicitly out of scope
- Full FFP-style compliance rules or board-patience objectives — deferred until the core loop is validated

## Exit criteria
A club's available transfer budget visibly increases following a strong run of squad share performance, traceable to specific dividend entries.
